About Me

Hey, I'm Imanuel Adi Condro — a designer and video editor passionate about creating clean, impactful, and purposeful visuals. I work with Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Figma to bring ideas to life through graphic design, illustration, UI design, video editing, and motion graphics. For me, great design is more than just looking good — it's about telling a story, delivering the right message, and making a real impact. That's why I take the time to understand each client's needs and project goals before getting started, making sure every piece of work looks great and serves a purpose.
